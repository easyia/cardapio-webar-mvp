# UI/UX Design & Human-Centered Experience Skill

## Core Directives for Luxury & High-Conversion Digital Products

### 1. The Anti-Generic "AI Look" Rulebook
- **NEVER** use generic futuristic purple-to-cyan neon gradients, random glowing orbs, or standard AI tropes.
- **DO** use grounded, tactile, organic, and editorial luxury palettes:
  - Deep rich undertones (Espresso Obsidian, Smoked Charcoal, Warm Parchment, Burnt Ochre, Botanical Sage).
  - Subtle noise/grain overlays and glassmorphism with delicate 1px borders (`border-white/10` or `border-amber-500/20`).
  - Warm lighting rather than cold artificial blues.

### 2. Spatial & WebAR Micro-Interactions
- Seamless transitions between 2D cards, 3D interactive canvases, and native AR projection.
- Real-world physics: spring damping on modals, drag-to-rotate inertia, tactile feedback on touch.
- Uncluttered HUD overlays that respect the 3D model's framing and lighting.

### 3. Visual Hierarchy & Spacing Rhythm
- Strict 8pt spatial grid system (4px, 8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 64px).
- Dynamic type scale with optical balance between Display Serifs and Clean Grotesque interfaces.
- Generous whitespace that conveys confidence, exclusivity, and craft.
