# Persuasive Design & Menu Engineering Skill

## 1. Gastronomy Behavioral Economics
- **The Golden Triangle**: Eyes naturally gravitate to:
  1. Center of the viewport (Hero / 3D Showcase)
  2. Top Right (High-margin signature items)
  3. Top Left (Appetizers / Entry items)
- **Pain of Paying Reduction**:
  - Format prices with elegance (e.g. `24,00` or subtle `R$ 24,00` instead of aggressive `$24.00!`).
  - Clear item breakdowns with transparent portion sizes and origins.
- **Sensory & Terroir Copywriting**:
  - Describe sensory attributes (mouthfeel, acidity, aroma notes, roasting process, temperature) rather than generic superlatives.
  - Highlight origin ("Serra da Mantiqueira", "Fermentação Natural 36h", "Bourbon Amarelo").

## 2. Decoy Effect & Tiered Choice Architecture
- Anchor high-value items next to signature items to make standard price points feel like incredible value.
- Clear distinction between Chef Specials, House Signatures, and Dietary Options (Vegan, Gluten-Free, Zero Sugar).
