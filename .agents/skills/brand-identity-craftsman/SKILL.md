# Brand Identity Craftsman Skill

## 1. Brand Architecture Principles
- A brand must possess a distinct soul, voice, and tactile aesthetic identity.
- Reject cookie-cutter "AI templates" in favor of handcrafted typography, bespoke iconography, and intentional color harmonies.

## 2. Typographic Pairings for Modern Luxury
- **Display / Editorial**: High-contrast, elegant serifs (`Playfair Display`, `Cormorant`, `Fraunces`, `Cinzel`) conveying craftsmanship and culinary pedigree.
- **Body & UI**: Neutral, geometric neo-grotesque (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, `Geist`) ensuring crisp readability on mobile retina screens.
- **Data & Micro-typography**: Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`, tabular figures) for prices, preparation times, and metrics.

## 3. Tactile Visual Language
- Warm organic surfaces: Dark roasted slate, warm travertine, brass/amber metallic accents, smoked amber glass.
- Clean vector emblems instead of generic emojis.
